What to do now

Three kinds of cleanup: change any passwords that leaked, delete the chats you’d rather not keep, and switch off training on your conversations.

Change these 17 passwords and keys first. Deleting a chat doesn’t take back a password that was already shared — it keeps working until you change it. Every row in the table above says where to do that.

ChatGPT

  1. Delete sensitive conversations: go to chatgpt.com, click your profile picture → Settings → Data controls → Delete all chats. (Or delete single chats from the list on the left.)
  2. Stop your chats being used for training: Settings → Data controls → turn off “Improve the model for everyone”.
  3. Ask OpenAI to delete your data: privacy.openai.com.

Claude

  1. Delete sensitive conversations: on claude.ai, hover over a chat in the sidebar and choose Delete. (Settings → Privacy can delete everything at once.)
  2. Check your training preference: Settings → Privacy → “Help improve Claude”.
  3. Ask Anthropic to delete your data: privacy.anthropic.com.

Claude Code (files on this computer)

  1. These are files saved on your own computer, in the .claude/projects folder in your home folder. Deleting a session file removes that history.
  2. More important: any password or key that appears in them may also have been sent to the AI while you worked. Change those first — the table above says how.

Gemini

  1. Delete your activity: myactivity.google.com/product/gemini.
  2. Set it to auto-delete: on the same page, “Choose an auto-delete option” (every 3, 18, or 36 months).
  3. Turn off Gemini Apps Activity if you don’t want conversations saved at all.

Files you scanned

  1. These came from files you pointed the scanner at — edit or delete them wherever they live.
